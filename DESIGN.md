# Design

## World

Lavender-romantic vintage stationery: a quiet paper ritual inspired by old letters, pressed petals, and night skies.

## Palette

- Lavender: #C8B6E2
- Pale lilac: #E6D9F2
- Warm ivory: #FFF9F5
- Powder rose: #EBCBD2
- Deep plum: #655078
- Muted gold: #D8BD91

## Typography

Elegant serif for the letter body, with a handwritten face for the title, intimate details, and signature.

## Surface

The closed envelope occupies the first viewport. Opening reveals warm ivory paper and shifts the scene into a vertically readable letter. The final scene closes the ritual with a quiet line and replay action.

## Motion

The focal moment is the sealed envelope opening in 3D, followed by a gentle camera-like lift and reveal. Ambient motion remains slow and sparse.
