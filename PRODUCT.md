# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide React. The implementation is delegated to the assistant.

## Users

A person receiving a private, romantic digital letter. The primary situation is opening a personal link and reading a heartfelt message.

## Product Purpose

Create an intimate, memorable letter-reading experience with a realistic opening sequence, a calm reading mode, and optional music.

## Positioning

The experience is a bespoke physical-letter ritual rather than a conventional landing page or card-based message.

## Operating Context

Runs in a mobile or desktop browser, beginning with a closed envelope and continuing through reading and a closing scene.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The letter, title, closing, and music source must be easy to configure.
- Opening is intentionally authored, 3D, and replay-safe.
- No music may play before explicit user interaction.
- The supplied written message must preserve its paragraphs and not be replaced by invented copy.
- The experience must support keyboard use, reduced motion, and responsive layouts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "A Letter for You"
- Lavender, pastel, vintage, romantic, dreamy, and quiet.
- Warm ivory paper with a softly aged envelope and wax seal.
- No commercial, generic, or childish presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete visual direction and supplied letter text are present in the project briefing attachment. The song must use a legitimate, authorized source.

## Product Principles

- Make the envelope and opening ritual the emotional center.
- Preserve reading comfort over ornamental complexity.
- Keep motion fluid, bounded, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Make all controls genuine and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard activation, visible focus, semantic labels, readable text, and motion preferences.
